💡 Surprise Finding
⚡
The data revealed a genuinely surprising fact: winning the toss offers almost zero advantage — toss winners win only 50.5% of matches (virtually a coin flip), yet teams that out-score in the death overs (16–20) win at a dramatically higher rate, with winners averaging 10.51 runs/over in that phase versus losers' 8.73 — a gap of 1.78 runs/over, far larger than in any other phase. Death-over dominance, not toss luck, is the real separator.
